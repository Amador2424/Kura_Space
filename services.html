About project estimates

Project times are estimates provided for planning purposes and are not guaranteed completion times. Every home and project is unique, and the actual time needed may vary based on:

Volume of belongings
Sorting & decluttering required
Client decision-making pace
Accessibility of the space & layout
Furniture or product assembly/setup
Unexpected conditions
Additions or changes to scope

If we anticipate that your project will require additional time beyond the estimated range, we will pause to review the progress, remaining work and expected additional time with you. Additional hours will only be continued with your approval.
