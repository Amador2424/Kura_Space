About Kura Space

The name Kura reflects its meaning — renewal and refresh — and that feeling is at the heart of everything we do.

OUR STORY

Our name reflects our purpose.

At Kura Space, we believe you don't need a brand-new home to experience a fresh start. Sometimes, all it takes is a thoughtful perspective, intentional organization, and a well-designed space to completely transform the way your home feels.

We believe your space should not only look beautiful but also support your routines, bring a sense of calm, and make everyday living feel more effortless.

Every project begins with understanding your lifestyle, routines, and goals. From customized organizing systems and strategic furniture rearranging to unpacking, decorating guidance, and virtual organizing, we create personalized solutions that bring lasting function, beauty, and ease to your home.

Whether you're transforming a single room or your entire home, Kura Space is here to create a space that truly supports the way you live.

Why choose Kura Space?

Every service is thoughtfully designed to provide more than an organized home — we create spaces that feel peaceful, functional, and tailored to the way you live.

  • Personalized organizing systems
  • Judgment-free support
  • Thoughtful decluttering guidance
  • Furniture rearranging when it improves your space
  • Product recommendations without unnecessary spending
  • Professional sourcing and procurement
  • Donation assistance
  • Maintenance strategies after your session
MEET THE FOUNDER

A lifelong love of organizing.

My love for organizing began long before I knew it could become a career. As a child, I loved surprising my family by organizing and rearranging different rooms while they were away. Seeing their excitement when they walked into a refreshed, calmer, more functional space quickly became one of my greatest joys. I naturally found myself creating order, improving layouts, and making spaces feel lighter using what we already had.

That passion eventually grew into Kura Space. Today, I help clients transform overwhelming spaces into homes that feel peaceful, intentional, and beautifully organized. My goal isn't simply to organize your belongings — it's to create personalized systems that support your everyday life and give you a renewed sense of calm every time you walk through your door.

Because sometimes, the biggest transformation begins with a fresh perspective.
