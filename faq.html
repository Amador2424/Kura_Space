FAQS

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before booking your complimentary consultation.

Do I need to buy organizing products first?

No. We always begin by maximizing what you already own. If additional products would improve the functionality of your space, we'll recommend and source them for you.

Will I have to throw my things away?

Not at all. We'll guide you through the decluttering process with compassion and without pressure. You remain in control of every decision.

Do you rearrange furniture?

Yes. If rearranging your furniture will improve the function and flow of your space, it's something we thoughtfully incorporate into your project.

Do you offer virtual services?

Yes. Our Virtual Organizing service allows us to guide and support clients anywhere.

What areas do you serve?

In-home services are available within our service area, with virtual organizing available nationwide.
